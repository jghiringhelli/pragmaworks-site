An afternoon of conversation gets you something you wouldn't have built any other way.

Generative Specification is the discipline that lets AI-assisted development run end-to-end without drift — without someone reading every line of generated code to catch the AI's mistakes. The idea: the spec is the work; the code is what derives from it. State your intent precisely enough and the formal tradition that took two and a half millennia to build — type systems, contracts, lattices, session types — runs against your project automatically.

The cookbook is the practical side. You don't need to read the white paper to use it — pick the recipe that matches your situation, follow the prompts, and your AI assistant will walk you through the rest. If you want to go deeper after, the links at the bottom take you to the theory.
