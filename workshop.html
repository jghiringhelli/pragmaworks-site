The one fair objection — and the answer inverts it. GS does not spend tokens; it saves them. The reason a session burns through a token budget is that, without authored structure, the AI re-reads the codebase and re-derives the architecture every single time. The spec, the sentinel, and the navigation tree are a compact map the AI reads once instead of re-discovering — so it pulls hundreds of tokens, not thousands. La única objeción justa — y la respuesta la da vuelta. GS no gasta tokens; los ahorra.
